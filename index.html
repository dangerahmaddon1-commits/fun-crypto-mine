Getting started

How It Works

1. Create an account

Register with Firebase email/password or Google sign-in to access your member workspace.

2. Open Mining Hub

Activate a free one-hour virtual session. Only one session can run at a time.

3. Wait for completion

The server tracks the session. You may close the page and return after one hour.

4. Collect points

A completed session credits 10 simulated points to your account. Start another session after the previous one completes.

5. Explore future rewards

Surveys, sponsored tasks, offerwalls and rewarded ads are Coming Soon until approved partners and funding are in place.

6. Withdrawals

Crypto withdrawals are not enabled. Points are not USDT and have no cash value. A future withdrawal feature requires verified funding and approval.

!
Advertising may support website operations. Do not click ads to earn points. Any future rewarded-ad placement must be explicitly approved by its provider.

Operations console

Admin overview

!
Admin security is enforced server-side. Access requires the verified owner Google account and a separate server-verified password. Elevated sessions expire after 15 minutes, and Firestore rules enforce that expiry.

Owner-only manual request queues are available here. No balance calculation, payment collection or automatic payout is connected. Production financial administration still requires stronger operational controls, multi-factor authentication, immutable audit logs and separation of duties.
